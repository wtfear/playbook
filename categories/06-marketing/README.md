# Category 06: Marketing

## 1. Purpose
Defines how the agency builds brand reputation, public visibility, authority, and inbound credibility through content marketing, case studies, social media, portfolio assets, and digital presence.

## 2. Scope
- Brand messaging, visual identity standards, and agency voice.
- Website copy, landing pages, and web asset maintenance.
- Case study creation, client story documentation, and proof-point development.
- Technical thought leadership (blog articles, guides, white papers, newsletters).
- Social media distribution (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, industry forums).
- Portfolio maintenance and public project showcase curation.
- Event participation collateral (speaker decks, webinar assets).
- Marketing analytics, conversion tracking, and monthly performance reviews.

## 3. Out of Scope (Boundaries)
- **Direct Outbound Prospecting:** Building prospect lists, cold email sequences, and 1-on-1 direct outreach belongs to [`../02-client-acquisition/`](../02-client-acquisition/README.md).
- **Direct Sales Pitches & Proposals:** 1-on-1 client discovery, customized proposals, and closing belong to [`../05-sales/`](../05-sales/README.md).
- **Core Strategy & Market Definition:** Determining which services to offer and strategic positioning belong to [`../03-business-strategy-and-governance/`](../03-business-strategy-and-governance/README.md).
- **Marketing Spend Approvals:** Approving advertising budgets, event sponsorships, or tooling fees belongs to [`../10-finance-and-money-management/`](../10-finance-and-money-management/README.md).

## 4. Related Categories
- [`../02-client-acquisition/`](../02-client-acquisition/README.md): Receives inbound marketing leads and uses marketing content for outbound campaigns.
- [`../05-sales/`](../05-sales/README.md): Uses published case studies, pitch decks, and proof points to accelerate sales cycles.
- [`../11-project-delivery-and-client-service/`](../11-project-delivery-and-client-service/README.md): Sources project success metrics, deliverables, and client quotes for case studies.
- [`../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/`](../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/README.md): Reviews client NDAs and approvals before public release of client names, logos, or case studies.

## 5. Candidate Playbooks and Workflows to Investigate
*(All items below are proposed candidates for future research and discussion; none are approved yet).*

- **[Proposed Workflow] Case Study Production & Client Approval Workflow:** Systematic process for interviewing delivery leads, drafting narratives, and securing written client sign-off.
- **[Proposed Workflow] Content Publishing & Distribution Workflow:** Editorial workflow from topic ideation, drafting, peer review, SEO check, to multi-channel syndication.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Brand Voice & Communication Style Playbook:** Guidelines on tone, terminology, technical clarity, and visual presentation.
- **[Proposed Checklist] Pre-Publishing Legal & NDA Verification Checklist:** Verification checks ensuring no confidential client data or proprietary code is publicly revealed.

## 6. Important Handoffs
- **From Business Strategy (03) → Marketing (06):** Target market positioning, core service catalog, and value propositions.
- **From Project Delivery (11) → Marketing (06):** Project completion alerts, client performance metrics, and candidate case studies.
- **From Marketing (06) → Client Acquisition (02):** Inbound inquiries and event attendee lists.
- **From Marketing (06) → Sales (05):** Sales enablement collateral (one-pagers, presentation templates, case studies).
