# Category 10: Finance and Money Management

## 1. Purpose
Defines the financial governance, cash planning, expense controls, invoicing, payment tracking, profitability analysis, and reserve policies that safeguard the agency's solvency, liquidity, and fiscal health.

## 2. Scope
- Agency operational budgeting and financial modeling.
- Cash flow forecasting, runway tracking, and burn rate management.
- Expense authorization policies, spending thresholds, and corporate card rules.
- Client invoicing (deposits, milestone billings, retainer cycles, final invoices).
- Accounts receivable (AR), payment tracking, and overdue collections escalation.
- Contractor compensation disbursements and payroll coordination.
- Operating cash reserve standards and capital retention rules.
- Engagement profitability analysis and gross margin monitoring.
- Banking relationships, payment processor setups (Stripe, bank wires).
- Coordination with external certified public accountants (CPAs) and tax advisors.

## 3. Out of Scope (Boundaries)
- **Sole Source of Financial Authority:** Finance **exclusively owns** spending controls, bank authorizations, and invoice issuance. Other categories (e.g., Sales, Engineering) must reference these controls and cannot invent independent financial rules.
- **Commercial Proposal Negotiations:** Day-to-day proposal pitching and client negotiation belongs to [`../05-sales/`](../05-sales/README.md), but must comply with Finance's margin floors.
- **Statutory Corporate Tax Filings:** Formal legal entity registrations and statutory tax filings are coordinated with external licensed professionals via [`../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/`](../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/README.md).

## 5. Candidate Playbooks and Workflows to Investigate
*(All items below are proposed candidates for future research and discussion; none are approved yet).*

- **[Proposed Policy] Expense Approval & Spending Threshold Policy:** Non-negotiable limits on who can authorize spending across different financial tiers.
- **[Proposed Workflow] Client Invoicing & Collections Workflow:** Exact sequence from milestone completion, invoice issuance, reminder cadences, to late fee or work-stop escalations.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Cash Runway & Emergency Reserve Playbook:** Principles for establishing and maintaining minimum operational cash buffers (e.g., 3-6 months of operating expenses).
- **[Proposed Workflow] Contractor Payment Coordination Workflow:** Bi-weekly or monthly schedule for reviewing, approving, and releasing contractor disbursements.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Engagement Profitability Analysis Playbook:** Framework for evaluating actual vs. estimated hours, contractor costs, and net margin per client.

## 6. Important Handoffs
- **From Sales (05) → Finance (10):** Executed contracts and billing contact details for deposit invoicing.
- **From Delivery (11) → Finance (10):** Written client milestone sign-offs triggering subsequent invoice generation.
- **From Finance (10) → Delivery (11):** Payment status notifications (specifically alerting delivery leads if an overdue invoice requires pausing work).
- **From Finance (10) → Strategy (03):** Monthly P&L, balance sheet, cash balance, and runway reports.

## 7. Open Questions
- What accounting software (e.g., Xero, QuickBooks) and business banking provider will be selected?
- What are the specific dollar thresholds for founder spend approvals (e.g., purchases under $X vs. above $X)?
- What is the default payment term policy for clients (e.g., Due on receipt for deposits, Net-14 for invoices)?
- What formal policy governs client work stoppage when invoices are delinquent past 15 or 30 days?
- What external CPA / tax advisory firm will be retained for annual filings?
