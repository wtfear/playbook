# Category 09: Internal Operations and Work Management

## 1. Purpose
Defines how everyday work within the agency is planned, prioritized, assigned, coordinated, and executed. It establishes the internal operating cadences, meeting hygiene, communication standards, and administrative workflows that keep the business running smoothly.

## 2. Scope
- Daily and weekly planning and retrospective rhythms.
- Task management workflows, issue tracking, and work-in-progress (WIP) limits.
- Task ownership, accountability conventions, and prioritization frameworks.
- Internal communication norms (asynchronous vs. synchronous, response time expectations).
- Meeting cadence, calendar hygiene, and agenda requirements.
- Team capacity allocation across client projects versus internal agency initiatives.
- Blocker identification, escalation, and resolution mechanisms.
- Recurring operational administration checklists (software renewals, routine updates).

## 3. Out of Scope (Boundaries)

## 5. Candidate Playbooks and Workflows to Investigate
*(All items below are proposed candidates for future research and discussion; none are approved yet).*

- **[Proposed Workflow] Weekly Operating Cadence Workflow:** Weekly sequence: Monday priority alignment, mid-week async check-ins, and Friday review/retrospective.
- **[Proposed Policy] Internal Communication & Asynchronous Collaboration Policy:** Response time expectations, notification etiquette, and documentation-first communication norms.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Meeting Hygiene & Decision-Making Playbook:** Standards for when to hold a meeting, mandatory written agendas, and meeting note documentation.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Blocker Triage & Escalation Playbook:** Rapid process for surfacing and unblocking stalled tasks within 24 hours.
- **[Proposed Checklist] Monthly Recurring Administrative Operations Checklist:** Verification list for routine operations, SaaS license audits, and administrative maintenance.
