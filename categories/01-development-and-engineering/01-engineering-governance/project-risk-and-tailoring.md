# Project Risk and Engineering Tailoring

> [!NOTE]
> **Candidate Practice / Proposed Scaffolding:** This document contains candidate material for research and evaluation. It does not constitute approved agency policy, mandatory instruction, or contractual commitment until formally reviewed and marked with `status: approved`.


## 1. Purpose
Establishes a proposed methodology for evaluating project technical risk and tailoring engineering standards, quality gates, and evidence requirements appropriately to the engagement context.

## 2. Proposed Scope
- Qualitative assessment of project risk factors (data sensitivity, business impact, external integration complexity, availability, and client compliance requirements).
- Definition of proposed risk tiers (Tier 1: Lightweight/Prototype, Tier 2: Standard Commercial, Tier 3: High-Assurance/Critical).
- Mapping of mandatory versus optional engineering standards across tiers.
- Procedures for documenting tailoring decisions in project kickoffs.

## 3. Applicability
Applies to all incoming technical engagements during pre-sales scoping (Lifecycle Stage 01) and technical kickoff (Lifecycle Stage 06).

- How can risk tailoring be performed quickly during pre-sales discovery without creating heavy administrative delays?
- What triggers an automatic elevation of a project from Tier 1 or 2 to Tier 3 during active development?
