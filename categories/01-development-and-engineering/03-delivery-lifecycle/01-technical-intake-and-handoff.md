# Lifecycle 01: Technical Intake and Pre-Sales Handoff

> [!NOTE]
> **Candidate Practice / Proposed Scaffolding:** This document contains candidate material for research and evaluation. It does not constitute approved agency policy, mandatory instruction, or contractual commitment until formally reviewed and marked with `status: approved`.


## 1. Purpose
Defines the proposed process for receiving prospective client requirements from Sales, conducting initial technical feasibility triage, identifying critical risks, and deciding whether to proceed with deep technical scoping.

## 2. Proposed Scope
- Initial technical qualification criteria (feasibility, team skill alignment, timeline realism).
- High-level risk triage and preliminary project risk tier assignment (Tier 1, 2, or 3).
- Identification of non-standard client technical constraints (legacy databases, specialized compliance, proprietary hardware).
- Formulation of initial technical assumptions, architectural risks, and exclusion boundaries for Sales proposals.
- Handoff from pre-sales commercial discussion to formal technical discovery.

## 3. Applicability
Applies to all incoming commercial opportunities with a software, automation, or infrastructure component before binding commercial proposals are issued.
