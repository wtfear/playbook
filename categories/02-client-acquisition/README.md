# Category 02: Client Acquisition (CA)

> [!NOTE]
> In this repository and agency context, **"CA" stands strictly for Client Acquisition** (not Chartered Accountant).

## 1. Purpose
Defines the strategies, workflows, and standards for identifying target accounts, conducting prospect research, executing outbound outreach, fostering strategic referral partnerships, and generating a predictable pipeline of qualified commercial opportunities.

## 2. Scope
- Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) targeting and account list building.
- Prospect intelligence, research, and contact discovery.
- Outbound outreach strategies (cold email, LinkedIn, warm introductions).
- Referral partner identification, partner co-marketing, and alliance building.
- Event networking and conference outreach workflows.
- Inbound lead triage, initial engagement, and qualification.
- Account-Based Marketing (ABM) campaign coordination with Marketing.
- Handoff of qualified prospect meetings to Sales.

## 3. Out of Scope (Boundaries)
- **Brand Awareness & Content Creation:** Publishing case studies, social media content, and maintaining public web visibility belongs to [`../06-marketing/`](../06-marketing/README.md).
- **Opportunity Evaluation & Closing:** Running in-depth discovery calls, pricing estimation, proposal writing, contract negotiation, and closing belongs to [`../05-sales/`](../05-sales/README.md).
- **Service & Market Strategy:** Determining the agency's primary offerings and strategic business model belongs to [`../03-business-strategy-and-governance/`](../03-business-strategy-and-governance/README.md).
- **Outreach Budget Authorization:** Approving spend on prospecting tools, data enrichment platforms, or list vendors belongs to [`../10-finance-and-money-management/`](../10-finance-and-money-management/README.md).

## 4. Related Categories
- [`../06-marketing/`](../06-marketing/README.md): Provides inbound collateral, portfolio assets, and marketing-generated leads.
- [`../05-sales/`](../05-sales/README.md): Receives qualified prospects for commercial discovery, proposals, and negotiation.
- [`../03-business-strategy-and-governance/`](../03-business-strategy-and-governance/README.md): Provides definitions of high-margin service offerings and target buyer personas.

## 5. Candidate Playbooks and Workflows to Investigate
*(All items below are proposed candidates for future research and discussion; none are approved yet).*

- **[Proposed Workflow] Account Research & List Building Workflow:** Systematic process for identifying, validating, and enriching target prospect accounts against the ICP.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Multi-Touch Outbound Outreach Playbook:** Best practices and ethical messaging frameworks for email, LinkedIn, and phone outreach.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Referral & Partner Channel Playbook:** Structuring mutually beneficial relationships with complementary agencies, freelancers, and software vendors.
- **[Proposed Workflow] Lead Triage & Sales Qualification Handoff Workflow:** Standardized operational flow for transitioning an interested prospect to a Sales discovery meeting.
- **[Proposed Checklist] Prospect Data Hygiene & Privacy Compliance Checklist:** Steps to ensure prospect contact info complies with applicable anti-spam and privacy regulations.

## 6. Important Handoffs
- **From Business Strategy (03) → Client Acquisition (02):** Validated service offerings, target pricing thresholds, and ICP criteria.
- **From Marketing (06) → Client Acquisition (02):** Inbound inquiries and event attendee lists for outbound follow-up.
- **From Client Acquisition (02) → Sales (05):** Qualified prospect package: verified interest, prospect background, business context, and scheduled discovery call.
- **From Sales (05) → Client Acquisition (02):** Disqualification feedback loop (explaining why specific prospects were not viable).

## 7. Open Questions
- What are the precise buyer personas (e.g., CTOs, Product Leads, Operations Directors) and target company revenue bands for our initial launch?
- What tools will be evaluated for CRM and outbound prospecting, and what are their cost vs. privacy implications?
- What concrete qualification criteria (e.g., BANT, CHAMP, or a custom agency scorecard) must be satisfied before a lead is handed off to Sales?
- Are referral commissions or rev-share fees permitted, and if so, how are they governed?
