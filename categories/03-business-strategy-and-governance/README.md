# Category 03: Business Strategy and Governance

## 1. Purpose
Defines the agency's commercial business model, core service offerings, market positioning, high-level objectives, founder roles, governance structures, and formal decision-making authority.

## 2. Scope
- Agency business model and commercial viability architecture.
- Core service offering catalog definition and lifecycle (introduction, expansion, retirement).
- Market positioning, value proposition, and competitive differentiation.
- Strategic goal setting (e.g., OKRs, annual targets, quarterly priorities).
- Founder roles, responsibilities, and decision-making rights (governance framework).
- Capital allocation priorities and reinvestment strategy.
- Strategic business reviews (Quarterly Business Reviews, annual strategy resets).
- Strategic risk evaluation and partnership governance.

## 3. Out of Scope (Boundaries)
- **Everyday Execution & Task Planning:** Daily and weekly task management, standups, sprint cadences, and internal communication rhythms belong to [`../09-internal-operations-and-work-management/`](../09-internal-operations-and-work-management/README.md).
- **Accounting & Financial Reporting:** P&L statements, tax filings, cash flow monitoring, and expense approvals are governed by [`../10-finance-and-money-management/`](../10-finance-and-money-management/README.md).
- **Statutory Corporate Formalities:** Formal corporate entity governance, shareholding agreements, and formal equity vesting contracts belong to [`../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/`](../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/README.md).
- **Brand Messaging & Campaign Execution:** Tactical marketing campaigns and creative copywriting belong to [`../06-marketing/`](../06-marketing/README.md).

## 5. Candidate Playbooks and Workflows to Investigate
*(All items below are proposed candidates for future research and discussion; none are approved yet).*

- **[Proposed Policy] Founder Decision Rights & Authority Matrix:** Explicit definition of which decisions require unanimous founder consent versus delegated individual ownership.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Quarterly Strategic Review (QSR) Playbook:** Cadence and agenda for evaluating quarterly progress, reassessing market feedback, and resetting priorities.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Service Offering Definition & Validation Playbook:** Systematic framework for piloting, packaging, and launching a new agency service.
- **[Proposed Workflow] Strategic Goal Setting & Alignment Workflow:** Process for defining and disseminating 90-day agency objectives across functional areas.

## 6. Important Handoffs
- **From Business Strategy (03) → All Categories:** Approved annual/quarterly objectives, target service catalog, and explicit decision boundaries.
- **From Finance (10) → Business Strategy (03):** Monthly and quarterly financial performance reports, margin analysis, and burn rate forecasts.
- **From Sales (05) & Project Delivery (11) → Business Strategy (03):** Client feedback, lost-deal analyses, and emergent market requirements to guide strategic pivots.

## 7. Open Questions
- What is our exact initial commercial model: custom software development, specialized automation retainers, productized service tiers, or consulting?
- How are ownership and primary accountability for the 13 categories divided among the founding team?
- What are the formal spending, contracting, and hiring thresholds requiring unanimous founder sign-off?
- How often will strategic priorities be reviewed and modified?
