# Category 05: Sales

## 1. Purpose
Defines how the agency qualifies commercial opportunities, conducts in-depth client discovery, prepares accurate project estimates and proposals, negotiates terms, overcomes objections, closes signed contracts, and seamlessly hands over new accounts to the delivery team.

## 2. Scope
- Commercial opportunity qualification and deal stage management.
- Discovery call framework and requirement elicitation.
- Technical estimation coordination (partnering with Engineering).
- Commercial pricing application (fixed-bid, retainer, T&M, value-based).
- Proposal and Statement of Work (SOW) drafting and presentation.
- Client pitch deck preparation and presentation delivery.
- Commercial negotiation, scope trade-offs, and objection handling.
- Closing workflows (securing verbal agreement, legal sign-off, deposit invoice).
- Formal sales-to-delivery handoff meetings.

## 3. Out of Scope (Boundaries)
- **Top-of-Funnel Lead Generation:** Outbound prospecting, cold email, list building, and initial meeting setting belong to [`../02-client-acquisition/`](../02-client-acquisition/README.md).
- **Public Collateral & Case Studies:** Developing public portfolio items and brand collateral belongs to [`../06-marketing/`](../06-marketing/README.md).
- **Project Kickoff & Execution:** Post-signature milestone management, delivery scheduling, and client project management belong to [`../11-project-delivery-and-client-service/`](../11-project-delivery-and-client-service/README.md).
- **Financial Terms & Margin Rules:** Minimum margin thresholds, payment term boundaries, and bank verification are governed by [`../10-finance-and-money-management/`](../10-finance-and-money-management/README.md).
- **Contract Legal Terms:** Non-standard legal terms, indemnification clauses, and MSA templates are governed by [`../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/`](../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/README.md).

## 6. Important Handoffs
- **From Client Acquisition (02) → Sales (05):** Qualified prospect dossier with background research and confirmed meeting schedule.
- **From Sales (05) → Engineering (01):** Scoping brief for technical feasibility and work-breakdown estimation.
- **From Engineering (01) → Sales (05):** Technical sizing, architectural risks, and deliverable milestones.
- **From Sales (05) → Project Delivery (11):** Completed Sales-to-Delivery Handoff Document, signed SOW, client stakeholders list, and context notes.
- **From Sales (05) → Finance (10):** Executed contract details for deposit invoice issuance and revenue tracking.

## 7. Open Questions

- Are deposits required before work commences in all cases (e.g., 50% upfront)?
