# Category 07: Personal and Professional Growth

## 1. Purpose
Defines how individuals within the agency—founders, leaders, and contributors—intentionally build capability, professional judgment, technical craft, communication clarity, leadership capacity, and resilience through deliberate practice, scenario role-play, and self-reflection.

## 2. Scope
- Individual technical capability advancement and skill-deepening frameworks.
- Executive communication, presentation skills, and client presence.
- Scenario practice and role-playing (e.g., tough client negotiations, discovery calls, incident escalation).
- Leadership judgment, emotional regulation, and decision-making under uncertainty.
- Constructive feedback exchange habits and receptive listening skills.
- Personal reflection, goal-setting, and burnout prevention routines.
- Knowledge-sharing sessions and internal brown-bag learning.

## 3. Out of Scope (Boundaries)
- **Organizational HR & Performance Reviews:** Formal employment appraisals, disciplinary actions, and compensation reviews belong to [`../04-people-and-relationship-management/`](../04-people-and-relationship-management/README.md).
- **Professional Development Spend Approvals:** Formal budget allocation, conference ticket reimbursements, and course fee authorizations are governed by [`../10-finance-and-money-management/`](../10-finance-and-money-management/README.md).
- **Operational Sprint Deadlines:** Day-to-day workload scheduling and delivery constraints belong to [`../09-internal-operations-and-work-management/`](../09-internal-operations-and-work-management/README.md).

## 5. Candidate Playbooks and Workflows to Investigate

- **[Proposed Playbook] Scenario Practice & Client Role-Play Playbook:** Rehearsing difficult client scenarios (e.g., missed deadlines, scope pushback, budget objections) in a safe environment.
- **[Proposed Workflow] Deliberate Technical Practice Workflow:** Framework for rapid exploration of new libraries, tools, and paradigms through scoped spikes.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Giving and Receiving Direct Feedback Playbook:** Practical techniques for radical candor, depersonalizing critique, and action-oriented feedback.
- **[Proposed Checklist] Personal Growth & Reflection Checklist:** Monthly self-audit of progress against individual professional development goals.
