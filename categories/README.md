# Functional Responsibility Categories

This directory contains the 13 foundational operating categories of the agency.

## Organization Principles

1. **Responsibility Areas, Not Rigid Departments:** These 13 categories represent functional operational responsibilities, not dedicated headcount or departmental silos. In an early-stage startup or boutique agency, a single founder or team member will typically own multiple areas simultaneously.
2. **Preserved Taxonomy:** All 13 categories are established as our permanent working taxonomy. They must not be silently merged, removed, or renamed.
3. **Stable Numeric Prefixes:** Category folders use fixed two-digit prefixes (`01-` through `13-`) to maintain stable sort order and clear cross-referencing across all documents and links.
4. **Separation from Support Folders:** Operational documentation (policies, playbooks, workflows, checklists) lives inside these category folders. Central support functions (decisions, central research, meeting records, templates, archives) live in dedicated top-level directories.

## Core Boundary Rules

To avoid confusion, duplication, and conflicting procedures across categories, the following operational boundaries are enforced:

- **Marketing vs. Client Acquisition vs. Sales:**
  - *Marketing* builds reputation, visibility, and inbound credibility.
  - *Client Acquisition* actively identifies prospects and initiates qualified conversations.
  - *Sales* qualifies opportunities commercially, estimates scope, negotiates terms, and closes signed commitments.
- **Business Strategy vs. Internal Operations:**
  - *Business Strategy* sets high-level objectives, offering definitions, and decision rights.
  - *Internal Operations* establishes daily and weekly execution rhythms, meeting structures, and capacity management.
- **Development & Engineering vs. Project Delivery:**
  - *Development & Engineering* owns technical implementation, architectural integrity, and code quality.
  - *Project Delivery* owns client communication, milestone schedule, timeline, scope changes, and budget tracking.
- **People Management vs. Personal Growth:**
  - *People Management* defines the agency's obligations to workers (contracts, onboarding, reviews, performance).
  - *Personal Growth* focuses on the individual's self-directed skill mastery, leadership, and deliberate practice.
- **Finance Ownership:**
  - *Finance* owns money controls, spend limits, invoicing policies, and cash reserve rules. Other categories reference these controls rather than redefining them.
- **End-to-End Category Scope:**
  - *End-to-End* orchestrates cross-functional workflows that stitch multiple categories together; it never duplicates functional details or acts as a catch-all junk drawer.
- **Knowledge Governance vs. Content Ownership:**
  - *Knowledge, Systems, and Automation* owns documentation formats, standards, and repository maintenance.
  - Each functional category directly owns the factual accuracy and currency of its operational documents.
