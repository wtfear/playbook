# Category 04: People and Relationship Management

## 1. Purpose
Defines how the agency attracts, onboards, supports, aligns, and manages talent (employees, contractors, and specialists), and how healthy, professional relationships are cultivated with internal team members, external partners, and key stakeholders.

## 2. Scope
- Role definition, hiring criteria, and talent acquisition.
- Contractor and freelancer sourcing, evaluation, and engagement models.
- Structured employee and contractor onboarding processes.
- Internal team collaboration norms, working agreements, and feedback mechanisms.
- Performance management, regular check-ins (1-on-1s), and performance reviews.
- Conflict management, escalation, and resolution protocols.
- Offboarding workflows, knowledge transfer, and access revocation coordination.
- Agency-wide relationship guidelines for dealing with clients, subcontractors, and alumni.

## 3. Out of Scope (Boundaries)
- **Individual Learning & Skill Mastery:** Self-directed study, personal skill advancement, communication drills, and technical practice belong to [`../07-personal-and-professional-growth/`](../07-personal-and-professional-growth/README.md).
- **Payroll & Compensation Disbursements:** Calculating tax withholdings, issuing payroll, and paying contractor invoices belong to [`../10-finance-and-money-management/`](../10-finance-and-money-management/README.md).
- **Employment Contracts & Legal Compliance:** Formal employment agreements, IP assignment contracts, non-disclosure agreements, and labor law compliance belong to [`../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/`](../12-legal-security-and-risk-management/README.md).
- **Daily Task Allocation:** Assigning daily sprint tasks and managing operational capacity belongs to [`../09-internal-operations-and-work-management/`](../09-internal-operations-and-work-management/README.md).

## 5. Candidate Playbooks and Workflows to Investigate
*(All items below are proposed candidates for future research and discussion; none are approved yet).*

- **[Proposed Workflow] Contractor Sourcing & Vetting Workflow:** Standardized process for sourcing, interviewing, testing, and selecting technical contractors.
- **[Proposed Workflow] New Hire & Contractor Onboarding Workflow:** Multi-day structured onboarding covering culture, tools, security training, and project context.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Continuous Feedback & 1-on-1 Playbook:** Framework for conducting candid, constructive bi-weekly 1-on-1s and feedback loops.
- **[Proposed Playbook] Performance Management & Underperformance Playbook:** Clear, fair, and documented process for addressing performance issues.
- **[Proposed Checklist] Offboarding & Security Handover Checklist:** Verification checks ensuring work handoff, equipment return, and complete credential revocation.

## 6. Important Handoffs
- **From Business Strategy (03) / Internal Ops (09) → People Management (04):** Staffing requirements and contractor capacity requests.
- **From People Management (04) → Legal (12):** Candidate details for NDA, IP assignment, and contractor agreement generation.
- **From People Management (04) → Finance (10):** Approved compensation rates and banking/payment details.
- **From People Management (04) → Personal Growth (07):** Developmental goals and training recommendations from performance reviews.

## 7. Open Questions
- What will be our initial ratio of core founders to contractors vs. full-time hires?
- What are the standardized hourly or project-based rate bands for contractor engagement?
- What constitutes an unacceptable cultural or professional breach warranting immediate contract termination?
- How will remote contractor performance and accountability be monitored without micromanagement?
