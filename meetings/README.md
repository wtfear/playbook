# Meeting Notes

This directory stores records of internal agency discussions, strategy syncs, category deep-dives, and operational reviews.

## Purpose

Documenting discussions, consensus, open questions, and explicit action items ensures internal alignment, prevents repeated discussions, and provides a clear audit trail of evolving operational thinking.

## Conventions

- **Template:** Use [`../templates/meeting-note-template.md`](../templates/meeting-note-template.md).
- **Naming Pattern:** `YYYY-MM-DD-[meeting-topic-slug].md` (e.g., `2026-11-20-category-01-scoping.md`).
- **Storage Rule:** All general, strategic, and cross-functional meeting notes belong here.
- **Action Tracking:** Every meeting note must clearly list assigned task owners and deadlines. Where a meeting results in a policy or architectural choice, an Operating Decision Record should be initiated in [`../decisions/`](../decisions/README.md).
